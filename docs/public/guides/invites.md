There are two kinds of invites on FluxVale. One gets you onto the platform. The other gets collaborators into your organization.

## Platform invitations

FluxVale is in invitation-only early access. You cannot create an account without one.

To get invited, write to [support@fluxvale.com](mailto:support@fluxvale.com). We send the invitation to your email address. The invitation email carries a one-time accept link:

- The link works once.
- It expires in 7 days.
- Accepting signs you in straight away and creates the account on the spot.

If an invitation expired or the link was already used, ask for a fresh one. Signing in with your email code alone does not create an account while the invite-only phase lasts. An invited address that tries the code flow is told to use its invitation link instead.

## Organization member invites

Organizations hold shared apps and a shared wallet. If you own one, you can invite members:

1. Open the organization's settings and go to **Members**.
2. Enter an email address and send the invite.
3. The member receives a link and joins on acceptance.

Members are either owners or plain members. Owners can manage billing, members, and everything else. Members work with the organization's apps. Invitations expire after 7 days and can be revoked before then.

A personal organization cannot be shared. It is yours alone, which is also why it has no members tab.

## Limits

You can own up to 10 organizations. There is no limit on how many organizations invite you as a member.
