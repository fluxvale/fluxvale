FluxVale is in invitation-only early access. Every account starts with an invitation.

## Get invited

Ask for an invitation by writing to [support@fluxvale.com](mailto:support@fluxvale.com). We will send an invite to your email address.

The invitation email carries a one-time accept link. The link expires in 7 days and works once. Opening it and accepting signs you in straight away, and your account is created on the spot.

## Sign in

FluxVale uses no passwords. Sign in with a one-time code:

1. Enter your email address at the sign-in page.
2. We email you a 6-digit code. It expires in 10 minutes.
3. Enter the code and you are signed in.

Sessions last 60 days on a device. There is no password to forget and none to leak.

## What an account includes

Your account comes with a personal organization and its own wallet. New accounts start with a small welcome credit so you can deploy something before you ever top up.

Apps you deploy can live in your personal organization or in a separate organization you create. Organizations have their own wallet, their own members, and their own apps. You can own up to 10 organizations.

## Account settings

Open **Settings** from the user menu to manage your account:

- **Profile** holds your handle. Renaming releases the old handle for anyone to claim, so choose carefully.
- **Access** holds the private-access gateway settings for your personal organization.
- **Developer** holds the developer tools for your account: approve FluxVale CLI device sign-ins, and create or revoke the API tokens your automation uses.
- **Email addresses** lists the addresses you can sign in with. Add more, verify them, set a primary, remove old ones.

Each extra email address must be verified with a sign-in code before it works.

## Get help

For anything about your account, write to [support@fluxvale.com](mailto:support@fluxvale.com). The [feedback board](https://fluxvale.com/feedback) is public and the [FAQ](/docs/help/faq) answers common questions.
