Auto-recharge reminders email your organization when the wallet balance dips below a threshold you choose. The email carries a one-click link that opens the top-up wizard with your amount pre-filled. You stay in control: FluxVale never charges a payment method on its own — topping up is always your decision.

## Turn reminders on

Open the billing page of your organization — or of your personal workspace; it works the same — and find the auto-recharge section. Only owners can change it.

1. Set the **threshold** — the balance that triggers a reminder.
2. Set the **amount** — what the one-click link pre-fills.
3. Switch reminders on.

## What the threshold and amount mean

- **Threshold** (default $5, minimum $1): when your balance drops below this, a reminder goes out.
- **Amount** (default $25): the top-up amount the email's link pre-fills. It follows the same $5 minimum as any top-up.

## How often reminders arrive

At most once a day while the balance stays below the threshold. Not per hour, not per dip — one email a day is the ceiling.

Top up above the threshold and the reminders stop. The next dip starts a fresh cycle.

## Turn reminders off

Switch reminders off in the same billing page section. No more emails come.

## Reminders are not the platform's warnings

The reminder is your preference. The platform's own balance protections run regardless of it:

- The platform watches every wallet on its own schedule: low-balance warnings, apps stopping when the balance reaches zero, and the 30-day data window with emails at 7 days and 1 day before deletion. These are platform-managed and unchanged by your reminder setting.
- A reminder email can land late or go unread. The platform's warnings and the stop ladder still apply underneath.

What happens when a wallet runs empty is described in [the FAQ](/docs/help/faq).
